I guess I’ve always been a slightly-above-average artist. My college art teacher\* apparently recognized the modest talent I had.

And I guess I’ve always liked watching birds - even though I was slightly afraid of the Killdeer that seemed to be following me as I walked through my grandfather’s fields on my way home. (I was maybe 10.)

It took me several decades to discover that I could combine pencil drawing with birdwatching. As of this writing, I’ve still only completed 3 colored pencil drawings of birds.
But it’s a start. (I’m only in my 60s.)

All of these first attempts are based on actual photographs of birds that I’ve seen. The first is a Blue Jay, the next is a Sandhill Crane, and the last is a House Finch.

As you’ll see below, I tend to draw the male of the species because he’s usually more colorful than the female. Since I’m using colored pencils, it only makes sense to make this choice.

## Blue Jay

For this drawing (and for the House Finch below), I used the method that I think many artists use: I traced the photo onto the drawing paper using carbon paper and special tape that’s easy to remove when you’re done tracing.

The Blue Jay actually was perched on a shepherd’s hook feeder pole. The brownish background I just kinda made up.

## Sandhill Crane

This crane has a different artistic story.

The bird itself started as a photograph, but I asked ChatGPT to turn that into a coloring book style image and change the background. So, if you look close enough, you’ll see lots of black lines everywhere.

I didn’t just shade in those areas like a 6-year-old might do. Instead I shaded each area either as I knew it looked like from the photo (for the bird) or as I imagined it might look like (for the background).

I think it turned out rather nice, considering the method used.

## House Finch

Both the finch and the background in this drawing are close to the original image - the bird being much more so. I took more liberties with the grassy background.

<img
  src="/images/articles/house-finch-drawing.jpg"
  alt="Colored pencil drawing of a House Finch"
/>

I plan to keep drawing birds from photos I’ve taken. It turns out I really do enjoy creating this kind of art. Maybe I’ll use some more advanced coloring techniques after I get a little better at the basics.

Don’t hold your breath though.
