## International Crane Foundation - A Zoo for Cranes

Living in Wisconsin, we are privileged to be close to the [International Crane Foundation](https://savingcranes.org/). It’s a little over an hour from home.

<img
  src="/images/articles/crane-takeoff-sculpture.jpeg"
  alt="Crane takeoff sculpture at International Crane Foundation entrance"
/>

It’s really a zoo solely for cranes – all 15 species that exist in the world.

We saw 13 of the 15. The other two – the Eurasian and the Black-necked – didn’t poke their heads out of their manmade houses for us.

### 1 SANDHILL CRANE

This is the only species I had seen before. They’re relatively common in Wisconsin in summer. This one wasn’t really sleeping. He had his eyes open most of the time. I just happened to press the button when he was blinking, I guess.

## And a Swallow

In the Whooping Crane Alcove, I heard and then saw this Cliff Swallow in its nest. I will be adding him to my life list.

<img src="/images/articles/cliff-swallow.jpeg" alt="Cliff Swallow in nest" />

Technically, you’re not supposed to add birds you see in zoos to your birding life list. I think the Crane Foundation counts as a zoo, so I won’t add the 12 new species of cranes I saw there to my official list, but this swallow was in the wild, so he counts!

We’d been considering this crane trip for some time. I’m very glad we finally accomplished it.
