Okay, you’re right. I didn’t actually try to give a robin a bath. But if I did….

This is really an account of what an American Robin sometimes does when he uses a bird bath.

## Stage 1 - The Perch

First, he’ll situate himself on the rim of the bird bath.

<img
  src="/images/articles/robin-on-bath-edge.JPG"
  alt="An American Robin standing on the edge of a bird bath"
/>

Perhaps he’ll take a look around to make sure no one is watching.

Or maybe he’s looking to make sure someone _is_ watching.

## Stage 2 - The Plunge

When he’s satisfied with his audience, or lack thereof, he’ll take the plunge, so to speak. One can’t really plunge into 3 inches of bird bath water. (Hm, maybe a hummingbird could.)

<img
  src="/images/articles/robin-bathing.JPG"
  alt="An American Robin flapping in a bird bath"
/>

How wet can a robin get in just a few inches of liquid?
