## Backyard Moment

Mourning Doves, House Finches, and House Sparrows are so common in my backyard that I tend to ignore them and look eagerly for those that don’t show up quite as often or in such quantities.

A tine Ruby-throated Hummingbird is always a welcome sight. So is the raucous Blue Jay and quiet Downy Woodpecker.

A quiet woodpecker?

Yes, actually I only hear him when he’s up in a tree relatively far away. When he visits the suet feeder, he’s quiet as a mouse, so to speak.

The other day, I think he surprised a House Finch. Take a look…

If I had to pick one, I’d probably choose the House Sparrow, though the House Finch would be a close second.

## From the Backyard Bird Tracker

I wouldn’t be able to tell you the following fact if I hadn’t been using my Backyard Bird Tracker app.

I haven’t seen an American Robin in my backyard (or anywhere else, for that matter) since August 7. That’s just over 2 weeks.

Now, maybe I’ve just not been looking at the right time, but I’m pretty sure the robins are making themselves scarce, staying closer to their homes.

The List Controls in the Recent Sightings section of the app lets me filter the list to just show American Robin (or any species) sightings. That’s how I can easily see which days I’ve spotted a robin.

Without the app, if you’d have asked me when I last saw a robin, I would probably have said, “Oh, last week, I guess.”

And I’d have been wrong.
