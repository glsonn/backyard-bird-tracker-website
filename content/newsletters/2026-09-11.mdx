## This Week in the Backyard

It’s one of those times of the year when, if you’ve been watching for more than a week or so, you notice some significant changes in the backyard.

Some of those changes may have to do with bird migration. If you’re interested in what may be going on in your backyard relative to migration, you might want to read this:

[Where Do the Birds Go?](https://www.backyardbirdtracker.com/articles/where-do-the-birds-go)

## What to Watch For This Week

So speaking of migration, this week see if you can identify a species that’s probably just passing through your area.

It could be just someone flying overhead, like a flock of geese or a pair of Sandhill Cranes.
