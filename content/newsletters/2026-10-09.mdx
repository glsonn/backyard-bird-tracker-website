Since this is the first year I’ve been keeping close tabs on the birds I see from day to day (using the app, of course), I was kinda expecting to see fewer birds as we moved into fall.

I was wrong.

I’ve been seeing more Mourning Doves, more Blue Jays, and more House Finches, just to name a few.

What about you?

## This Week in the Backyard

It’s almost getting hard to keep up with the Blue Jays. I’ve now got 3 of them working my peanut feeder fairly regularly.

Lately I’ve been able to refill the almost-empty ring feeder every other day.

I’ve noticed that the jays aren’t always flying far away to hide the nuts. Sometimes I think they’re just finding a nearby branch where they can eat the peanuts in peace and safety.

I did get a decent shot of one from my feeder cam.

## What to Watch For This Week

Watch changes in familiar birds.

Pay attention to the birds you see every day. Are they still arriving at the same time? Are they spending more time feeding? Are some birds becoming less common? My robins and hummers are long gone.

Sometimes the easiest way to notice migration is to notice that your backyard regulars aren't quite acting like they did a few weeks ago.

## Backyard Moment

Once in a while the best moments in birding are really unexpected.

I recently saw a murder of 4 crows on the horizontal bar at the top of a streetlight across the road. I’ve seen 3 crows a few times before, so this fourth one was a bit of a surprise.

I saw them one day, and then the next day 2 were atop the light, one was atop a nearby, very tall evergreen, and another was atop a vent on the roof of a nearby apartment building. It almost looked like that last one was looking for a nesting place, but it’s not really the time of year for that. Maybe he’d found some food - bugs? - in the vent.

## Journal Prompt

What changed? What’s different about the birds in your yard compared with a month ago?

Maybe a familiar bird has disappeared (again, my hummers and robins), a new one has arrived (no juncos yet), or you're simply seeing fewer birds at certain times of day.
