I refer to the Guess Who song called No Time in the subject line because it feels so appropriate this week.

Some of our maple leaves are starting to turn a color other than green. My local Blue Jay has been a regular at the peanut feeder. (His feathers have grown back.) The temps are in the 60s instead of the 70s or 80s.

I do not wonder why.

## This Week in the Backyard

The hummingbird feeder is in storage until spring.

That Blue Jay has visited the peanut feeder at least 3 times as I write this. He used to give his imitation hawk cry before coming in for a landing. Now he doesn’t even bother. I guess he knows it’s his territory, and there’s little to no competition.

Fortunately, I have over half a bag of peanuts I can supply him with.

I’ve put some peanuts on the tray of the camera feeder to try to get a closeup picture of the jay. I’ve seen shots of him taking the nuts, but none are good enough for publication. I’ll be sure to include one here when I get a good one.

## What to Watch For This Week

Watch for new birds.

I realize that’s easier said than accomplished. You can watch for them all you want, but if they just don’t show up….

Still, be prepared for them and have your [Backyard Bird Tracker app](https://app.backyardbirdtracker.com/) handy.

## Perrot State Park, Take 2

My wife and I hiked to the top of Brady’s Bluff in Wisconsin’s Perrot State Park this past weekend. We made it to the top two years ago too.

The park and bluff are located along the Mississippi River, so the views from up there are awesome any time of year.

I heard multiple Blue Jays, White-breasted Nuthatches, Red-bellied Woodpeckers, and a few more along the way.

But I didn’t see any of them. Grr.

So, it was a great hiking but a sad birding trip. Maybe we’ll try again some year.
