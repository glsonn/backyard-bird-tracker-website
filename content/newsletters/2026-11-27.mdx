## This Week in the Backyard

Maybe, if you’re not retired yet, you have the whole weekend off. Maybe you’ll have more time to do a little more birding than usual. If so, make good use of it.

Maybe even take a few minutes to add [Backyard Bird Tracker](https://www.backyardbirdtracker.com/) to your collection of birding tools. Which, by the way, now has all 1000+ ABA bird species available for you to select. Only some have pictures, but that’s not the important part anyway.

Give it a whirl. I know you’ll be glad you did.

## What to Watch For This Week

Not so much what to watch for as where to watch from this week. Since it’s getting cooler outside and you probably don’t want to be out there without a few layers on, try just watching out your window.

The birds won’t care whether you’re out there with them or not.

## Backyard Moment

Have you ever seen something interesting (bird-related) while doing something completely unrelated?

Me? More than once.

I might just be walking to the store, not really caring about any birds that might be seen along the way, when suddenly there he is. A killdeer. A hawk. Or a pair of cranes.

Just so cool.

## Journal Prompt

What unexpected sighting(s) have you had? Write about one in your journal this week.

Haven’t started a journal yet? No time like the present. A cheap notebook from your local dollar store plus a gel pen and you’re all set.

## From the Backyard Bird Tracker

I’ve mentioned before that we don’t see all that many birds on most of our hiking trips. And that’s fine. You don’t need a formal birding outing to do your birding.

As I said above, just look out your window. If you haven’t actually done that in a while, you might be surprised at what you can see after just a few minutes.

If you don’t see anything at first, try again an hour later. I’ve noticed that my birds seem to all come at once and then all disappear at once for a while.

You can make notes about such activity in Backyard Bird Tracker, but only if you’ve “added” it to your kit. (There’s really nothing to add as such, since it’s just a website you’re accessing.)
